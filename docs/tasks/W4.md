# W4 — Adjustments and filters

> **Draft, Claude, 26 Sep 2026.** Checks: `npm run check` + the **Check**
> list in the browser (D-003). Decisions in W4-T00 first.

Goal:
- Photoshop's 16 adjustments, as adjustment layers and as Image ▸
  Adjustments, with the Properties panel live;
- the filter families people use, with a live preview and Fade;
- the automatic corrections.

Exit criteria:
- dragging a Curves point on a 4000 × 3000 photo redraws within a frame;
- a filter preview follows its slider;
- a Gaussian Blur 20 on 4000 × 3000 applies in ≤ 2 s with the interface live.

Read first:
- `fotox/crates/fx-core/src/layer.rs` (`Adjustment`, 14 kinds and their
  parameters);
- `fotox/crates/fx-render/src/adjust.rs` and the adjustment parts of
  `composite.wgsl` (the maths, including Hue/Saturation's six ranges);
- `fotox/crates/fx-ops/src/filter.rs` (tiles with an apron, edge replicate,
  D-036);
- BitMappery's `src/rendering/filters/*` and `src/workers/filter.worker.ts`;
- miniPaint's `src/js/modules/effects/*` (algorithms to port).

Order: T00 → T01 → T02 → T03 … T08 (any order) → T09 → T10.

## W4-T00 — Decisions (Rob)

1. **Adjustment layers render on the GPU** in the compositor (Fotox's WGSL,
   ported with W1). The same code applies Image ▸ Adjustments destructively
   (render into a surface). **Yes.**
2. **Filters run on CPU in a pool of job workers**, on regions with an apron,
   in plain TypeScript. A filter that turns out too slow is moved to a
   WebGPU compute shader in the same card. **Yes.**
3. **Preview**: the filter runs on the visible area only, at the view's
   scale, with distances scaled (Fotox M4-T05). The latest request wins.
   **Yes.**
4. **Which filters.** The families of T03–T08 (about 50 filters). Filter
   Gallery, Lens Correction, Camera Raw, Liquify and Vanishing Point are not
   here: Liquify and Vanishing Point are W9, and the other three are later if
   ever. **Yes.**

## W4-T01 — Adjustment layers and Image ▸ Adjustments

**Files**
- `src/core/adjustments/*.ts` (one per kind: parameters, defaults, presets);
- `src/engine/gpu/adjust.wgsl`;
- `ui/js/native/properties-panel.js`;
- `ui/js/native/adjustments-panel.js`.

**What to build**
- Kinds:
  - Brightness/Contrast (and Legacy), Levels, Curves, Exposure, Vibrance,
    Hue/Saturation (Colorize, the six ranges);
  - Color Balance, Black & White, Photo Filter, Channel Mixer;
  - Color Lookup (`.cube` / `.3dl`, the table in the document);
  - Invert, Posterize, Threshold, Gradient Map, Selective Color (Relative /
    Absolute).
- The Properties panel and the Adjustments panel are live. Presets are
  Photoshop's, where they are published.
- Levels / Curves have the eyedroppers and Auto. Hue/Saturation has the
  on-image drag.
- Image ▸ Adjustments ▸ … applies the same thing to the layer's pixels (one
  step), limited by the selection.
- Selective Color's and Black & White's formulas are published
  approximations, marked `VERIFY` for W12.

**Check**
- Stack Curves + Hue/Saturation + a Color Lookup over a photo.
- Drag the Curves points and watch it live.
- Mask an adjustment.
- Apply Levels destructively through a selection.

## W4-T02 — The filter framework

**Files**
- `src/filters/{registry.ts,types.ts}`;
- `src/jobs/filter.worker.ts`;
- `src/engine/filter-preview.ts`;
- `ui/js/native/filters.js` (Fotox's dialogs made live).

**What to build**
- A filter is a file:
  - its `ParamSpec`s (the dialog is generated from them unless the filter has
    its own);
  - `apron(params, scale)`;
  - `run(src: PixelBlock, params, scale) → PixelBlock`.
- The dialog shows the live preview on the canvas (T00-3) plus the small
  preview window Photoshop has.
- OK applies as a job, region by region with progress, limited by the
  selection (the result blended through its coverage), one history step with
  the filter's name.
- Filter ▸ Last Filter (Ctrl+F); Edit ▸ Fade (Shift+Ctrl+F: opacity + mode of
  the last filter or adjustment, as one step that replaces it).

**Check**: Gaussian Blur with the preview on, a selection-limited Unsharp
Mask, then Fade to 50 % Luminosity, then Ctrl+F.

## W4-T03 — Blur

**Files**
- `src/filters/blur/*.ts`.

**What to build**
- Gaussian Blur (separable; box-blur passes at large radii);
- Box Blur, Motion Blur (angle, distance);
- Radial Blur (spin / zoom, quality);
- Surface Blur (radius, threshold);
- Blur, Blur More, Average;
- Lens Blur (simplified: a disc kernel with bright-spot boost, the depth
  from a mask).

**Check**: each blur on a photo against Photoshop's at the same settings, by
eye.

## W4-T04 — Sharpen

**Files**
- `src/filters/sharpen/*.ts`.

**What to build**
- Unsharp Mask (amount, radius, threshold);
- Smart Sharpen (Gaussian / Lens / Motion removal, reduce noise, shadows and
  highlights fades, simplified);
- Sharpen, Sharpen More, Sharpen Edges;
- High Pass (under Other in Photoshop's menu, same code).

**Check**: Unsharp Mask 100 % / 1 px on a portrait matches Photoshop's look.

## W4-T05 — Noise

**Files**
- `src/filters/noise/*.ts`.

**What to build**
- Add Noise (uniform / Gaussian, monochromatic; seeded, so the preview
  equals the result);
- Median;
- Dust & Scratches;
- Despeckle;
- Reduce Noise (basic: strength, preserve details, colour noise).

**Check**: Dust & Scratches removes specks from a scanned photo; Add Noise's
preview equals the result.

## W4-T06 — Distort

**Files**
- `src/filters/distort/*.ts` (inverse mapping, bilinear or bicubic sampling).

**What to build**
- Pinch, Twirl, Spherize;
- Ripple, Wave, ZigZag;
- Polar Coordinates;
- Displace (a map from a file or an open document);
- Shear (curve).

**Check**: each on a checkerboard shows the expected geometry; Polar
Coordinates and back is close to the original.

## W4-T07 — Stylize and Pixelate

**Files**
- `src/filters/{stylize,pixelate}/*.ts`.

**What to build**
- Stylize:
  - Emboss, Find Edges, Glowing Edges;
  - Solarize, Diffuse, Oil Paint (stylization, cleanliness, scale, bristle,
    lighting);
  - Wind, Tiles, Trace Contour, Extrude (flat blocks).
- Pixelate: Mosaic, Crystallize, Color Halftone, Pointillize, Fragment.

**Check**: each on a photo; Oil Paint at defaults resembles Photoshop's.

## W4-T08 — Render and Other

**Files**
- `src/filters/{render,other}/*.ts`.

**What to build**
- Render:
  - Clouds and Difference Clouds (seeded, tiled seamlessly);
  - Fibers;
  - Lens Flare (the four lens types, simplified);
  - Lighting Effects (one to three lights with a bump channel, simplified).
- Other: Offset (wrap / repeat / background), Minimum, Maximum (with
  roundness), Custom (a 5 × 5 kernel), HSB/HSL conversion.

**Check**: Clouds uses the current colours; Offset with wrap around makes a
seamless texture check (useful for Rob's textures).

## W4-T09 — Automatic corrections and colour tools

**Files**
- `src/core/adjustments/auto.ts`;
- `src/filters/color/*.ts`.

**What to build**
- Image ▸ Auto Tone / Auto Contrast / Auto Color (Photoshop's clipping
  defaults, 0.1 %).
- Desaturate, Equalize, Match Color (between two documents), Replace Color
  (fuzziness, a selection preview).
- Shadows/Highlights (amount, tone, radius; the advanced options simplified).
- HDR Toning is left out.

**Check**: Auto Tone on an underexposed photo; Shadows/Highlights lifts the
shadows without halos at defaults.

from different families, compares them with Photoshop at the same settings
